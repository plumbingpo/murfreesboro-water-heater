Food Service Hot Water

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and an undersized or failing heater reveals itself as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and a backed-up dish room. A high-temperature dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater working alongside the main supply. Our food-service work covers high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. If the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine can't hit its final rinse at peak service. We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, keeping downtime short, and keeping part of the system online whenever the layout allows.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide from a blocked or undersized vent, scalding at the tap, Legionella growth in water stored too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Murfreesboro Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and the permit and inspection process. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.

Preventive Care

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Murfreesboro Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, sediment flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve tests, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties on a program typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and more consistent water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
